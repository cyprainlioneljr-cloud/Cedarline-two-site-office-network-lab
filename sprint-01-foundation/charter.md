# Cedarline Branch Network Charter

Cedarline Distribution is opening a branch in Brno. Houston headquarters hosts Finance, Operations, IT, Security, and the internal inventory app. Brno Operations needs access to that app to check stock and process orders.

The lab will model the two offices in Cisco Packet Tracer. Houston will have separate department networks, staff and guest Wi-Fi, IP phones, a shared printer, and an entrance camera with PoE planned. Security will be able to view the camera. Only the designated IT admin workstation will be allowed to manage the switches.

Brno Operations will reach the Houston inventory app over a site-to-site IPsec VPN. Access to Finance workstations and switch management will be blocked. Work-from-home VPN implementation depends on what the installed simulator can demonstrate.

The lab runs locally at $0. The handoff will include the Packet Tracer file, floor plans, an IP and VLAN plan, a switch-port map, and an access matrix backed by actual test evidence.
